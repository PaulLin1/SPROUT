# Consolidated Catalog of Internet Visual Aesthetics

A working census of the named aesthetics in scope, organized by **affective operation on
the past** (the study's mechanism taxonomy) rather than by folk label. Nostalgia is the
through-line; the uncanny/eerie family is one *region* of it (its inversion), not the
center of gravity. Many aesthetics span two cells — overlaps are noted explicitly, since
those boundary cases (Frutiger Aero in revival, retro-futurism) are the theoretically
richest.

**Mechanism clusters**
- **R — Restorative nostalgia**: warm, comfort-seeking, techno-optimist; "the good past/
  future as it felt." Boym's *restorative* pole.
- **H — Hauntological**: ironic / melancholic; "lost futures," decay, the future that
  never arrived (Fisher). Reflective, ambivalent.
- **I — Inverted / uncanny**: nostalgia stripped of comfort; liminal, kenopsia, the
  eerie (Fisher: something absent that should be present).
- **A — Anemoia**: longing for a past one never lived (and that may never have existed).
- **C — Control**: present-day, non-nostalgic everyday baseline.

Valence key: ➕ positive · ➖ negative/unsettling · ⚖ ambivalent/bittersweet.

## Retro-futurism (the key dual-mechanism case)

Retro-futurism is the most theoretically valuable entry because it sits **across the R/H
boundary**, and which way it leans is exactly the variable the framework should measure:

- **Optimistic strand → Restorative (R).** Googie architecture, Raygun Gothic, World's-
  Fair atomic optimism, bright synthwave: "the future as a *promise*," rendered with
  confidence and gloss. Affectively continuous with Frutiger Aero's techno-optimism.
- **Melancholic strand → Hauntological (H).** Decayed atompunk, cassette futurism,
  faded space-age signage: "the future that *never arrived*"—Fisher's lost futures
  exactly. The same visual vocabulary (rockets, ray guns, chrome) read as ruin rather
  than promise.

This duality is a feature, not a problem: it gives a within-aesthetic test of whether the
restorative/hauntological split is recoverable from image features (gloss, colorfulness,
typicality, surprise) holding subject-matter roughly constant.

proper) are sparsely tagged in open-license banks, so they are approximated by their
visual constituents; authentic exemplars would require per-creator licensing or a
controlled generative arm. This is the honest cost of a licensed-only stimulus set.
